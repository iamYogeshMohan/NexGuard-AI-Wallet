01 Executive Architecture Summary

NexGuard addresses modern digital transaction vulnerabilities (account takeovers, mule syndicates, rapid velocity attacks, and session hijacking) by unifying continuous mobile telemetry with real-time distributed AI agents.

📱 Customer AI Wallet

Mobile-first fintech application providing ₹10,00,000 starting demo funds, biometric step-up challenges, instant QR Scan to Pay, 94/100 security score ring, device pairing, and emergency account lock.

🛡️ Bank SOC Control Center

Desktop command center for bank security operators, featuring live WebSocket transaction streams, 10 AI agent inspectability, incident timelines, copilot assistant, and one-click account freezing.

10 Orchestrator Consensus Synthesizes findings from all 9 agents, generates composite risk score (0–100), and issues final enforcement verdict. Consensus

04 Three-Tier Risk Verdict Framework

Verdict Risk Score Automated System Behavior Customer & SOC Experience
ALLOW 0 – 39 Instant execution against NG Bank ledger balance. Green checkmark in customer wallet. Live event broadcast to SOC as normal traffic.
VERIFY 40 – 74 Transaction paused. Step-Up challenge generated. Modal prompt for Biometric / OTP verification. Flagged in SOC transaction stream for review.
BLOCK 75 – 100 Transaction rejected. Security Incident created. Payment prevented; critical alert notification pushed to wallet; incident card created in SOC triage queue.
